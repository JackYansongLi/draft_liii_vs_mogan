\begin{abstract}
    As large language models (LLMs) increasingly assist scientific writing, limitations and the significant token cost of {\TeX} generating become more and more visible. This paper analyzes {\TeX}'s fundamental defects in lack of structural representation to illustrate its limitations on generated semantics and error localization. As an alternative, \textbf{Mogan STEM}, a low-entropy structured representation, is introduced. Mogan outperforms {\TeX} in the above aspects by its efficient data structure and clear contextual boundaries. Extensive experiments are conducted to verify Mogan's superior performance in LLM tasks. Furthermore, we show that due to Mogan's lower information entropy, it is more efficient to use \texttt{.tmu} (the document format of Mogan) to fine-tune LLMs than {\TeX}, achieving a 43\% reduction in training loss . Our work provides a more scalable and AI-friendly data representation in the era of AI4Science.
\end{abstract}
